# Verified Research Papers — Citation Integrity, Fabrication, Hallucination, and Generative Search

This section contains research papers relevant to citation fabrication, citation verification, hallucination, retrieval-augmented generation, and generative search. Some papers were reused from the AI-assisted research paper, while additional papers were selected to broaden the coverage of the topic.

## A. References reused from the original AI-generated research paper

### 1. Exploring the Boundaries of Reality: Investigating the Phenomenon of Artificial Intelligence Hallucination in Scientific Writing Through ChatGPT References

- **Year:** 2023
- **Venue:** Cureus, 15(4), e37432
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.7759/cureus.37432
- **Relevance:** Directly investigates hallucinated references generated by ChatGPT in scientific writing.

### 2. High Rates of Fabricated and Inaccurate References in ChatGPT-Generated Medical Content

- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.7759/cureus.39238

- **Venue:** The American Economist, 69(1), 80–87
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1177/05694345231218454

## Verification policy

Every item above was checked against an authoritative record such as a publisher page, PubMed/PMC, ACL Anthology, NeurIPS proceedings, ICLR proceedings, or arXiv. No intentionally fabricated papers or authors are included.

Where a paper is only a preprint, it is explicitly labeled **arXiv preprint** rather than being presented as peer-reviewed publication.
